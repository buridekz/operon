SterileVoice

A voice copilot for the operating room. It runs the WHO Surgical Safety Checklist by voice, reads back every critical event, guards against recorded allergies, and patches in a specialist, so the team never touches a screen.

Sample case: time-out with a skipped item → tourniquet read-back → allergy catch → "call vascular" → auto-drafted record.
